🧠 Level-Up Concepts
Beyond the Basics
01
Liquidity Sweeps
Price often runs above the Asia high or below the Asia low right at London open — not a breakout, but a hunt for stops. The real move frequently comes after the sweep, in the opposite direction.
02
Kill Zones vs Full Sessions
The full session is when market is "open." The kill zone is the narrow window inside it (first 2–3h) where most of the directional move happens — the rest is often drift or consolidation.
03
News Overrides Session Logic
CPI, NFP, FOMC can flip expected session behavior instantly. Always check the economic calendar before trusting the session pattern alone.
04
Spread Widens at Rollover
Around 00:00–01:00 EAT (NY close / broker rollover) spreads widen and price can spike on thin liquidity — a common source of stop-outs that have nothing to do with your analysis.
⚡ Session Rules
Quick Reference
Build your bias during Asia, execute in the London/NY prime overlap (16:00–20:00 EAT).
Treat the London AM sweep of the Asia range as a signal — it's a liquidity grab, not a breakout.
Check the economic calendar before trusting any session pattern today.
Size down or step aside during the 01:00–03:00 EAT dead zone.
Avoid chasing the first London move without confirming the Asia range was actually swept.
Avoid new entries in NY PM after the AM trend has already exhausted.
Never trade full size through scheduled high-impact news — session or no session.
NOTE: Session behavior is a baseline, not a guarantee. A geopolitical shock or central-bank surprise can override normal session patterns at any hour — always check headlines and the calendar alongside this map, not instead of it.
