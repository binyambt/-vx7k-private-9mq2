Executive Summary

Five questions, five honest answers — the rest of this page is the reasoning behind each one.

R&D, not income
Should you keep trading forex?
Yes — but as a skill-build project, not a paycheck. 70–90% of retail traders lose money long-term; only 5–7% stay consistently profitable after 5 years. Fund it with design income until your edge is verified.
Not your main bet
Should you pivot to full web development?
Junior dev hiring is down ~35% since 2023 globally. Keep AI vibe-coding as a support skill layered onto design — not a standalone career bet.
Start month 1
What's your fastest path to real money?
Graphic Design + AI fluency + Brand/UI systems, sold as productized services to international clients.
Highest 5-yr odds
What's the ONE path with the best odds?
"Design-Led Brand Systems Studio" — you do high-value creative/strategy work, hire juniors for execution, reinvest profit into trading capital and NibNova.
Genuine tailwind
Is Ethiopia's new FX policy good news for you?
Yes. Under the 2026 NBE directive, Ethiopian service exporters (IT, design, consulting) can retain 100% of foreign currency earnings indefinitely in a retention account — a real structural advantage that didn't exist two years ago.

    Graphic Design → Brand Identity Systems

    You already have a portfolio, a channel, and reps. Fastest to first paid client. AI hasn't killed strategic brand work — only "make it look decent" execution work.

    🥈

    UI/UX Design (light layer on design)

    Same visual-thinking muscle as graphic design, but 2–3x the hourly rate — one of the few design categories still growing (7–8%/year) while pure graphic design employment shrinks.

    🥉

    AI Vibe-Coding (Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable, Replit)

    A better fit for you than traditional no-code tools or hand-written development. Lets you sell "design + working site/app" as one package and sidesteps the saturated junior-coding market entirely.

    4

    AI Design Tools (Midjourney, Firefly, Figma AI)

    Not a separate career — a multiplier on #1–3. 75% of designers now use AI tools; job listings requiring AI fluency jumped from 3% to 32% in two years.

    5

    Digital Marketing (later)

    Natural extension once you have design + a brand — offer "design + the campaign that uses it" as a bundled retainer.

    🟡 Honest caution on vibe-coding: it's a real skill, not a shortcut. Clients will hit bugs, need a form that actually sends email, or want something outside the AI tool's defaults. Build the judgment to catch and fix wrong output — that's what separates "can prompt a website" from "can be trusted with a client's real project."

    🔴 Risk factors specific to you

    • 5 months of study is early — most who become consistent took 2–4 years and blew multiple accounts along the way
    • No verified profitable strategy yet — that's just where you are in the sample-collection process
    • No trading capital — actually protective right now, no way to blow up real savings while learning

    ✅ Why you shouldn't quit

    • Backtesting (not live trading) is the correct, nearly-free way to build edge
    • Prop firms offer $10K–$200K simulated capital, 70–95% profit split — no personal deposit at risk, once edge is verified
    • Trading is one of the only AI-resistant, unlimited-upside, no-degree skills on this list — if you get there

    🎯 Recommendation: Forex stays side R&D funded by design income — not a job — until two conditions are met: (1) 100+ backtested trades with verified positive expectancy, and (2) 3+ months of forward-testing that matches the backtest. Only then touch a prop firm challenge with real intent.
